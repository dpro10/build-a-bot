Calvin cycle (light-independent)

Happens in the STROMA of the chloroplast

Uses ATP + NADPH from the light reactions

3 steps:

1) Carbon fixation: RuBisCO sticks CO₂ onto RuBP

2) Reduction: ATP + NADPH → G3P (3-carbon sugar)

3) Regeneration: G3P rebuilds RuBP

3 turns → 1 G3P out (costs 9 ATP + 6 NADPH)

Light rxns = thylakoids → make O₂, ATP, NADPH
