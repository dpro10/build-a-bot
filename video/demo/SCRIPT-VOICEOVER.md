# Diego's voice-over: the script

For the cut without a narrator (`edit-voiceover.mjs`, 2:49, rendered to `video/out/demo/build-a-bot-demo-voiceover.mp4`). The picture, the music and the app's own sounds (the call, voice typing, the meeting) are done; this is what you say over it.

## How to record it (about 10 minutes)

1. **Somewhere quiet**, with soft things around (a closet, or a room with a bed and curtains). Turn off fans.
2. **Your phone is fine** (Voice Memos), or QuickTime on the Mac (File › New Audio Recording). Hold the mic about a hand's width from your mouth, a little to the side.
3. **Read the lines below top to bottom in one take**, like you're showing a friend. Leave a short breath between sentences, and a two-second pause between sections. If you trip, pause and say the sentence again; Claude keeps the last good one.
4. **Send the file** (any format). Claude cuts it into sentences, puts each one where it belongs, and mixes it with the music and the app's sounds.

You don't need to watch the video while you read: the timing is done for you. If you'd rather talk over the video live, play it with headphones on (so the mic only hears you) and record at the same time; that works too.

## Saying it your way

Change any line so it sounds like you. Keep the names that matter for the judges:

- **Nebius Token Factory** and **NVIDIA Nemotron**: the thinking.
- **NVIDIA Parakeet and Magpie**: hearing and speaking.
- **Tavily**: web search.

Keep each section about as long as its time; much longer and a part of the video gets held a little longer to fit.
