## Every claim, and where it's true

- **Gets smarter the more you use it:** memory, not training. Bots keep up to 2,000 memories and use the ones that matter for each message (docs/DECISIONS.md, "memory that works on any brain"; the Memory page says so). Nothing in the video says we train a model.
- **See, change, export, delete:** the Memory page edits and deletes memories; the Account page exports everything and deletes the account (`/legal/privacy` › Export, delete and your rights).
- **We never sell it, never train AI on it:** the privacy policy's own words ("We don't sell your data… or use it to train AI models, ours or anyone else's"). It's Build-a-bot's promise about what it holds, which is why the voice says "we".
- **Any brain, keeps its memory:** included open models (Nemotron 3.5 Lightning by default) on Nebius Token Factory, your own API key, or your Claude or ChatGPT plan through the desktop app; switching keeps memory and apps (the builder: "Switch anytime — its memory and apps stay put").
- **Open source:** Apache 2.0 since 2026-10-17 (docs/DECISIONS.md).
- **Zero data retention:** true for the included open models **only while Zero Data Retention is switched on for our Nebius Token Factory organization** (it's off by default: Token Factory otherwise keeps prompts and replies, in Finland, to speed up inference; research_notes/Build a bot hackathon upgrades/nebius_platform.md). The footnote says so. With your own API key or plan, that provider's terms apply, which is why the voice says "the open models".
- **Your voice is heard on your own computer:** voice typing and meeting notes run NVIDIA Parakeet in the desktop app, and the audio never reaches our servers (the privacy policy). The scene shows the desktop app, and the footnote names both features.
- **18+, always says it's an AI:** product rules (docs/DECISIONS.md), on the end card.
